PMS Integration: Your Competitive Advantage

🎯 The AirGate Difference: Unlike generic WiFi solutions, AirGate connects directly to your property management system via FIAS protocol. Guest credentials are automatically synchronized. Room assignments, check-in/check-out, and multiple guests per room are all handled seamlessly.

Automatic Guest Synchronization

When a guest checks in at your front desk, their WiFi credentials are automatically created and ready. No additional manual processes. When they check out, their access is automatically revoked. Your PMS and WiFi network stay perfectly synchronized in real-time.

Credential Format:
Username: Room Number + Guest Last Name
Example: 2002_Smith
Password:
Auto-generated secure password
Displayed to guest at check-in
Sync Speed:
Real-time
Instant activation at check-in

Multi-Guest Per Room Support

Hotels often have multiple guests in one room. AirGate assigns unique WiFi credentials to each guest in the same room (e.g., 2002_307, 2002_308). Each guest gets independent session management, independent billing/upsell, and separate connection tracking.

Smart Access Control

Room-based access policies ensure guests can only connect during their stay. Early check-ins and late check-outs are handled automatically. Emergency override options available for hotel staff.

Automatic Logout at Check-out

When a guest checks out, their WiFi session is automatically terminated. No lingering connections. Network bandwidth is freed up immediately for the next guest. Supports both on-time and late checkout scenarios.

How It Works - Guest Workflow

From check-in to WiFi connection - a seamless experience for your guests

1️⃣ Guest Check-In

Front Desk
Guest arrives and checks in using your PMS. Receptionist enters guest name and room number.

Example: John Smith → Room 2002

2️⃣ Automatic Sync

Real-Time Integration
AirGate instantly receives guest data from PMS and creates WiFi credentials automatically. Zero manual work.

✓ Credentials created in 1 second

3️⃣ Guest Goes to Room

Guest Experience
Guest reaches room and opens WiFi settings. Sees hotel's WiFi network available.

📶 WiFi Network: Hotel-Guest-WiFi

4️⃣ Enter Credentials

Simple Login
Guest enters auto-generated credentials using Room Number + Last Name format.

Username: 2002_Smith
Password: Auto-generated

5️⃣ WiFi Connected

Full Access
Guest enjoys high-speed WiFi with full access to hotel services and promotions.

✓ Full bandwidth access

6️⃣ Auto Checkout

Guest Leaves
When guest checks out, WiFi access is automatically revoked. No lingering connections.

✓ Zero manual intervention
